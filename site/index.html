AI-assisted inquiry into connectedness

An experimental site testing how ecological, social, and historical systems connect.

ConnectedNature investigates connectedness across nature, ecology, society, knowledge, history, and human systems. Each article begins from a statement or question, then moves through evidence, source evaluation, and synthesis rather than simply asserting an opinion.

  • Starts from an originating statement or question
  • Tests it against sources and competing interpretations
  • Distinguishes the starting proposition from the researched conclusion

How To Read A Page

Originating statement

The opening claim is the proposition being examined.

Researched conclusion

The conclusion is written only after evidence has been gathered and tested.

Method In Brief

From statement to synthesis

A page begins with a statement or question precise enough to investigate. It then gathers relevant material, evaluates what each source can actually support, compares serious interpretations, and ends with a synthesis that marks what is established, what remains plausible, and what stays open.

ConnectedNature And COIN

Public writing supported by a visible research workflow

ConnectedNature is the public site for AI-assisted inquiry. COIN is the research and provenance workflow behind it. The page is written for readers; COIN keeps the working trail visible through GitHub-based question logs, source registers, evaluation notes, and revisions.

AI may assist with search, comparison, and drafting, but interpretation remains under human critical guidance.

Evidence First, Currency Later

The fourth article asks what must be true before a financial instrument can credibly claim to be backed by nature. It treats Regenerative Finance (ReFi) as a live problem field, then argues that tokenized ecological claims need a deterministic, inspectable, and separately governed evidence layer upstream.

Research question

Can ecological limits become transaction-level constraints before governed evidence exists?

Inquiry path

Regenerative Finance (ReFi), ecological accounting, European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) evidence bundles, procedural decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) governance, and blockchain provenance.

What follows

A narrowed claim: evidence can become procedurally binding before it can prove planetary effectiveness.

Research Process

How an inquiry moves

The method is simple on purpose: frame the inquiry clearly, gather and test evidence, evaluate sources, and write a conclusion that stays proportionate to what the record can bear.

  1. Frame the inquiry. Define the statement or question precisely enough that evidence can answer it.
  2. Gather evidence. Bring together historical cases, ecological research, primary sources, and analytical work that bear on the question.
  3. Evaluate sources. Ask what each source can actually support, where its limits lie, and what biases may shape it.
  4. Synthesize critically. Write a conclusion that distinguishes what is established, what is plausible, and what remains open.
